# 🚖 Ola vs Uber Business Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

In this project, I performed an end-to-end comparative analysis of **Ola** and **Uber** to understand how the two platforms differ in terms of ride fulfillment, operational efficiency, pricing strategy, and customer experience.

Using **Python**, **SQL**, and **Power BI**, I transformed raw ride data into actionable business insights and built interactive dashboards to answer key business questions such as:

* Which platform completes more rides successfully?
* Which platform operates more efficiently?
* Who has the stronger pricing strategy?
* Which company provides a better customer experience?

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* Python (Pandas, NumPy)
* MySQL
* Power BI
* DAX

---

## 📂 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering (Python)

I cleaned and transformed the raw dataset by handling missing values, standardizing fields, and creating analytical features such as:

* Ride Outcome
* Time Bucket
* Day Type
* Vehicle Category
* Fare per KM

### 2. Business Analysis (SQL)

I wrote SQL queries to calculate KPIs and compare Ola and Uber across operations, pricing, customer behavior, and ride performance.

### 3. Dashboard Development (Power BI)

I designed four interactive dashboards:

#### Executive Overview

Provides a high-level comparison of ride fulfillment, pricing, customer satisfaction, and wait times.

#### Pricing Strategy Analysis

Evaluates fare structures, ride distances, customer spend, and revenue efficiency.

#### Operational Efficiency

Analyzes driver allocation speed, pickup delays, wait times, and cancellation behavior.

#### Customer Experience

Compares ratings, payment preferences, customer retention, and lifetime value.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* Ola achieves a higher ride fulfillment rate than Uber.
* Driver-side issues account for the majority of ride cancellations on both platforms.
* Uber matches drivers faster and maintains significantly lower customer wait times.
* Uber generates higher revenue per kilometer, indicating stronger pricing efficiency.
* Ola consistently receives higher customer and driver ratings.
* Uber customers demonstrate higher retention and lifetime value.

---

## 🎯 Final Conclusion

The analysis reveals a clear trade-off between the two platforms.

**Ola excels in customer satisfaction and ride completion performance**, while **Uber leads in operational efficiency, pricing effectiveness, and customer retention**.

These findings suggest that Ola should focus on improving dispatch efficiency and reducing wait times, whereas Uber should prioritize customer experience and ride completion rates.
